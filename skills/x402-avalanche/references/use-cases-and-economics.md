# Use Cases and Economics

## Traditional friction (summary)
Common problems of the classic payment stack:
- Long onboarding (accounts, KYC, keys, subscriptions).
- Fixed/percentage fees that break micropayments.
- Slow settlement (days).
- Poor compatibility with automation and AI agents.

## What x402 enables
- Per-request monetization without mandatory subscription.
- Viable micropayments in APIs and content.
- Autonomous payments between services/agents.
- Native HTTP integration with `402 Payment Required`.

## Why Avalanche fits well
- Fast finality (~1s on C-Chain).
- Low costs per tx (on the order of ~$0.001, depending on network conditions).
- EVM compatibility and broad tooling.
- Solid support for USDC and x402 facilitators.

## Typical use cases
- Premium data APIs (market, weather, signals).
- Granular content access (article/video/document).
- AI services with pay-per-actual-use.
- Agent-to-agent economy (m2m payments).

## When to read this file
- When justifying business/product.
- When designing pricing and GTM for monetized API.
- When explaining why x402 + Avalanche vs traditional payments.
